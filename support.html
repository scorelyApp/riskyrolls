Common questions

How do I remove ads?

Open a locked mode or the ad-free strip, then buy Premium Monthly or Premium once. Restore if you already paid on another device.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

Use Manage subscription on the Premium sheet, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Why don't my scores appear on the global board?

Sign in with Game Center and play Solo. Only signed-in Solo runs are submitted.

Ads didn't show / too many ads?

Ads wait until you finish a game, then play before the next one. Premium removes them entirely. If a region requires it, Privacy options on the home screen controls consent.
