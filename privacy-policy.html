Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Introduction

Risky Rolls (“we,” “us”) is a dice game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what leaves your device when you play. Scores and player names you type stay on the device unless you choose Game Center.

What stays on your device

Finished games, named players, and settings are stored on this device. Deleting the app removes that data from the device.

Game Center

Signing in with Game Center is optional. Apple handles that account. We use it only to submit Solo high scores to the leaderboards you see in Game Center.

Purchases

Apple processes every purchase. We receive product identifiers and whether Premium is active. We do not see your payment card or Apple ID password. Premium is either a monthly auto-renewing subscription or a one-time purchase; both remove ads and unlock Endless.

Ads

Free modes are supported by Google AdMob interstitial ads after a finished game. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use the device’s advertising identifier to measure ads. You can decline tracking and still play.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics receives anonymous gameplay events such as starting a mode. We do not send your name, Game Center ID, or other personal text in those events.

Children

Risky Rolls is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Decline App Tracking if you prefer not to be tracked.
  • In some regions a Privacy options control on the home screen lets you change ad consent.
  • Restore or manage purchases from the Premium sheet, or in iOS Settings under your Apple ID.
